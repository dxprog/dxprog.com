Two quick things:

1. I read a "life pro tip" forever ago that the TL;DR of a large body of text should be put at the top of said text. If somebody's not going to read the wall, they're never gonna make it to the summary. I do this at work all the time, and I guess I did it here... at least for the email, not for this blog post.
2. This letter seems... _prosier_ than usual? I like flexing creative my lexical abilities as much as the next person, but it seems I went a little farther here. Or I forgot some puncuation in places. Mayhaps both.

There's a lot to remark on in this letter, but also not a lot... because none of it is particularly remarkable. The goal of making 2025 personally "quieter" was more or less achieved. Life is about to hit cruising altitude in terms of new and big life goals, with most of the nuclear family checklist completed. Have a "career", am married, own a house (attached to the ground), and - in a few months - will have two kids, covering each set of genitals. Everything after that is maintenance mode until retirement, I suppose. But, enough mild depressive philosophizing.

## Run Stuff
The Disneyland run challenge of a 10k and half-marathon on subsequent days was completed just fine, even with little training going into it and actually running both in the middle of having a sinus infection that hadn't been diagnosed yet. The Bend half-marathon was also completed, which meant a nice little trip out to the central state and getting to visit with the devbus folks. When I did the Bend 10k back in 2019, I don't think I had any intentions of coming back to do the half-marathon, but now I have and that circle is complete. Adding to those, I rounded out the year with a local 10k and a holiday half-marathon in Portland. Currently, I have no further runs lined up, but the loose idea of doing the 2029 Disney World marathon...

## Bike Stuff
I definitely did more biking in 2025 than in years past, but nowhere at the level I set up in the letter. The scenic bike trail remains a goal to hit, but we did get a kiddo bike trailer in the summer. There were grandiose plans of using it to take Tweeb to day care in the morning, but it turns out biking a trailer up hills is difficult. There was a bit of time where I was doing research into an eBike to fix that issue, but eBikes and child trailers apparently don't play nicely together, so that was eventually dropped. Still, Tweeb and I made a few human-powered trips around the neighborhood, as did I by myself out into the wider area. All told, I biked about 150 miles, about the same distance I ran. Both of which are peanuts compared to the 700+ miles I walked.

## Childrens
When I wrote this email to myself last year, I believe we had _just_ signed the papers for day care. It was two day turn around from setting up a tour to getting the available slot, which was a little mind blowing considering we were anticipating a close to a year waitlist had we stayed in the Bay Area. But, that aside, day care's been great for everybody. Kalya and I have peace and quiet to get work done, and Tweeb has learned all sorts of stuff and made a bunch of little toddler friends and sometimes eats things other than chicken nuggets and raisins...

In the camp of "Baby 2", that kid has been realized. Not _fully_ as yet; she currently spends her days beating up Kayla's insides. And, yes, the sex can be inferred by that pronoun (or by what was explicitly stated up top). It was amazing how all the gender related stress just completely vanished upon getting the genetic test back. I'm very much ready to ride out the world of pink and princesses as long as we're able.

## Everything Else
Honestly, mostly skipping over the work related stuff. Didn't get promo'd, don't really care. I survived another giant round of layoffs, that saw a bunch of folks I was starting to get to know in the Portland office getting swept away. At this point, I'm doing my best to be content where I'm at. Gainful employment affords myself and my family nice things.

Re-reading my video production desires once again hits a sore spot. I thought I'd set myself up a reasonable challenge to produce only two videos of any kind. It's the one thing I want to do, and I've had a couple starts on getting at it, but either perfectionism gets in the way or lack of time or lack of energy. That being said, I completed a bunch of house DIY things, some of which were carry overs from 2024, so that's satisfying. Also, finally got all the 3D printer upgrades I'd been waiting on the move to do done, so that's also a thing. Hoping that between naps and such, I can get _something_ video related done during pat leave. Maybe I can start directing Tweeb in random little things.

Well, there it is. A couple days late, but another year's blog obligation satisfied. Someday, I'll quit doing these things.

Probably when the screed's become too similar.
